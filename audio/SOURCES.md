## Edits

- `cut-paper-shred.wav`: short shredder fragment, accelerated, filtered to reduce motor rumble, layered with a quiet slash, and normalized.
- `cut-plastic-crunch.wav`: bottle scrunch transient, accelerated and filtered, layered with a quiet slash, and normalized.
- `cut-metal-shear.wav`: short metal scrape layered with a metal clang, faded and normalized.
- `cut-glass-shatter.wav`: glass shatter shortened and softened, with a quiet slash onset.
- `cut-general-waste-slice.wav`: clean slash trimmed, filtered, faded, and normalized.

All output files are mono, 44.1 kHz, 16-bit PCM WAV for dependable browser playback.
